# DEMS realistic evidence pack (20 files)

All content is fictional, made for the capstone. Names, plates, amounts and places are invented. Scenarios follow common Philippine case types (theft/robbery, estafa and online fraud, grave threats, physical injuries, carnapping, reckless imprudence), and the evidence kinds match what A.M. No. 01-7-01-SC (Rules on Electronic Evidence) covers: photographs, video and audio recordings, electronic documents and ephemeral communications (chats, screenshots).

The audio files are synthetic stand-ins (tones shaped like a phone call), not real speech. Replace any file with your own real recording or photo if you want.
